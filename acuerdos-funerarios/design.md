# Design System — Acuerdos Funerarios SERFUNCOOP

## Identity
- App Name: Acuerdos Funerarios SERFUNCOOP
- Context: Funeral services agreement recording — dignified, professional, trustworthy

## Color Palette
- Background: #0A0A0A (near black)
- Surface: #141414
- Card: #1C1C1C
- Border: #2A2A2A
- Primary: #C9A84C (warm gold)
- Primary Light: #E2C97A
- Primary Dark: #9E7A28
- Text Primary: #F5F5F5
- Text Secondary: #9A9A9A
- Text Muted: #555555
- Danger: #C0392B
- Success: #27AE60
- Recording Red: #E74C3C

## Typography
- Font: Poppins (headings), System default fallback for body
- H1: 24px, Bold, #F5F5F5
- H2: 20px, SemiBold, #F5F5F5
- H3: 16px, SemiBold, #F5F5F5
- Body: 14px, Regular, #F5F5F5
- Caption: 12px, Regular, #9A9A9A
- Label: 11px, Medium, uppercase, #C9A84C

## Spacing
- xs: 4
- sm: 8
- md: 16
- lg: 24
- xl: 32
- xxl: 48

## Components
- Cards: background #1C1C1C, border #2A2A2A, borderRadius 12, padding 16
- Buttons Primary: background #C9A84C, text #0A0A0A, bold, borderRadius 10
- Buttons Secondary: border #C9A84C, text #C9A84C, transparent bg
- Buttons Danger: background #C0392B, text white
- Inputs: background #1C1C1C, border #2A2A2A, text #F5F5F5, placeholder #555555
- Status badges: rounded pill, small text

## Icons
- Use @expo/vector-icons (Ionicons, MaterialIcons)

## UX Patterns
- Bottom tab navigation: Inicio, Nueva Sesión, Historial
- Header: dark with gold accent
- Recording screen: full screen experience with pulsing red indicator
- Lists: flat, minimal, dark cards with gold accents on active states
- Forms: stacked inputs, gold focus borders
- PDF preview: modal overlay
